Our promise

Most consultants deliver a deck. We deliver a region that runs better. A Field Operations Lead from our team works full-time in your stores, and our senior team directs the work every week until the numbers move.

What we usually find

Great product, uneven store experience

When we read customer reviews across a network, taste is rarely the complaint. Almost every problem is operational, and every one has a fix that can be written down, trained and checked.

Slow service

Long waits for a single order, even when the store is quiet.

FixCrew scheduled to hourly traffic, with a speed target.

Cooking standards

Burnt or undercooked, cold food, dry buns.

FixCooking SOP backed by timers and line checks.

Crew attitude

Staff on phones or chatting at the counter.

FixA named shift lead on the floor, plus soft-skills coaching.

Order accuracy

Missing sauces, drinks or items. Wrong orders.

FixPacking checklist and a handover check.

Hygiene

Dirty floors, bare hands, tired frying oil.

FixDaily cleaning checklist and an oil-change log.

Honest hours

“Open 24 hours” on the listing, closed at the door.

FixListed hours that match real staffing, on every app.

A single bad experience can go viral and reach every outlet’s customers. That is why we put standards before sales: clean, safe, well-run stores protect the brand, and sales follow.

What we fix

Six workstreams, one standard

Most issues share one root cause: there is no single standard that every store, shift and level works to. We build that standard and make it stick.

01

Quality & audit

One QA standard, audit form and schedule for every store, company-owned and franchise alike, run by calibrated auditors.

02

One standard

One SOP library, short and visual, written in Bahasa. One weekly report that every store fills in the same way.

03

Labour & scheduling

Crew rostered to hourly traffic, overtime under control, and sales per labour hour tracked for every store.

04

Food cost & waste

Recipe costing, portion control, regular stock counts and waste logs, so actual food cost matches what the menu was priced on.

05

Training & coaching

A coaching cascade where each level coaches the one below, plus role-based leadership and business modules from our Academy.

06

New store openings

An opening playbook: handover checklist, crew hired and trained before day one, and close support through the first 90 days.

Questions you may have

Straight answers

We only have a few stores. Is this for us?

Yes. For a growing brand the pilot can cover your whole network, and the 4-week Health Check is a low-risk way to start.

How much of your time do we get?

The Field Operations Lead works full-time in your pilot stores. Our senior team directs the work, visits your stores in person every month, and joins every steering meeting.

Can you fire people?

No. We recommend, and you make the final call on any people decision.

Do you need experience in our exact format?

Running stores well works the same across formats: staffing to traffic, one standard, checked every day. Our track record running large store networks shows it.

What happens after the pilot?

You choose. Hire a permanent head of operations with our help on recruiting and handover, or extend the model to more stores while you decide.

Can we stop early?

Yes. Either side may exit after Month 3 with 30 days’ notice.

What do you need from us?

Access to sales, operations and audit data, one sponsor on your management team, a fortnightly steering meeting, and brand backing with franchise partners in the pilot.
