The problems that grow with every store you open

Every store does it differently

Each outlet has its own way of working, and nobody checks the same way twice.

Reviews are slipping

Slow service, wrong orders and a bad moment at the counter end up on Google and the delivery apps.

Food cost keeps creeping up

Portions drift, waste goes unrecorded, and margin disappears without anyone noticing.

Crew don’t match the rush

Too many people on shift when it’s quiet, too few at peak, and overtime keeps rising.

Good people leave

Crew and managers can’t see a path to grow, so your best ones walk out and take their know-how with them.

New stores open shaky

Every opening pulls your best people away, and the new store takes months to settle.

The root cause

Most of these come from one gap: nobody owns the standard in every store, every day.

Head office writes the SOPs. Store managers are busy running shifts. In between, standards slowly slip. That gap is where Project BM works.
